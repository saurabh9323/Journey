      Travelling with the baby

      7 adults need SSD tokens; the baby is extra.

      • The baby is about 1 year 1 month, so the infant darshan (Supadham) is for under 12 months only. Not eligible; treat any exception as a bonus.
      • Children under 12 need no SSD token. The baby goes in with the mother, who has her own token.
      • Ask 155257 whether an under-5 child darshan falls on Dec 1 or 2.
      • Avoid the free queue (12–15 hours). Carry the birth certificate, parents’ original Aadhaar, milk, water, diapers, wipes, spare clothes, medicines.
      • Trains: no berth for a child under 5, so the baby shares a parent’s berth. Confirm with the TTE.
      • Vehicles for 8 people: tempo traveller for Dhanushkodi and for Dec 2 (about ₹4,000–6,000, estimate). Skip the Alipiri footpath.
